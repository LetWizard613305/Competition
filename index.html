Introduction

Two powerful ideas that move the world forward

💡

What is Innovation?

Innovation is turning a fresh idea into something useful, whether it is a new product, service or way of solving a problem.

⚙️

What is Technology?

Technology is the set of tools, machines and knowledge we use to make life easier, faster and more connected.

🤝

Why They Go Together

Innovation supplies the ideas and technology makes them real. Together they drive progress in every field.

Evolution of Technology

From steam engines to artificial intelligence

1760s – Steam & Machines

The Industrial Revolution begins and factories replace hand production. Steam engines began powering factories, trains and ships in Britain, so goods could be made faster and in far larger numbers. Cities grew quickly as people moved from villages to find work, and travel and trade became easier, which laid the foundation for every later technological leap.

Late 1800s – Electricity

Electric power and the telephone change homes, industry and communication. Inventors like Thomas Edison, Nikola Tesla and Alexander Graham Bell brought electric light, power grids and long-distance calling into daily life. Factories could work longer hours, streets became safer at night, and electricity grew into the energy backbone of almost every later invention.

1940s – First Computers

Giant electronic computers like ENIAC begin to calculate at amazing speed. These early machines filled whole rooms and used thousands of vacuum tubes, yet they solved in seconds what people needed days to calculate. After the transistor was invented, computers became smaller, cheaper and faster, opening the door to business and personal computing.

1969 – Birth of the Internet

ARPANET connects computers, laying the base of today's internet. Funded by the United States Department of Defense, it sent its first message between two computers in California in 1969. More institutions joined over time, and shared rules for sending data, called protocols, were created, which grew into the global internet we use today.

1990s – World Wide Web

Websites and email bring information to ordinary people. In 1989 Tim Berners-Lee proposed the World Wide Web, and by the early 1990s browsers and websites were open to the public. Anyone could now read news, share ideas or send a message across the world within seconds, and search engines and online shopping soon followed.

2007 – Smartphones

Touchscreen phones put the internet in everyone's pocket. The first iPhone in 2007 combined a computer, camera, music player and web browser in a single device. App stores let developers build tools for banking, travel, learning and entertainment, and new industries like ride sharing, food delivery and mobile gaming grew rapidly around smartphones.

2010s – Cloud & Social Media

Data moves online, apps are everywhere and the world gets connected. Cloud computing lets people and companies store files and run software over the internet instead of on one computer. Social media connected billions of users, while streaming, video meetings and digital payments became normal, and large amounts of data began guiding smarter decisions.

2020s – Generative AI

AI tools that write, draw and code become part of daily life. Generative AI learns from huge amounts of data to create text, images, music and software code on request. Students, doctors, artists and businesses use it to save time and explore ideas, while society discusses responsible use, privacy and fairness so that everyone can benefit.

Impact on Our Future

Technology is changing every part of life

🏥 Healthcare

Technology revolutionizes healthcare by improving diagnostic accuracy, enabling remote telemedicine consultations, streamlining electronic health records, and allowing real-time patient monitoring through wearable devices, enhancing overall patient outcomes and operational efficiency

🎓 Education

Technology transforms education by expanding access to global information, enabling personalized self-paced learning, and fostering interactive collaboration through digital tools. It bridges geographical gaps, automates grading for teachers, and prepares students with essential digital skills for a tech-driven workforce.

🌱 Environment

Technology impacts the environment double-edgedly: it drives renewable energy advancements and smarter resource tracking, yet accelerates e-waste, high carbon emissions from data centres, and intensive resource extraction for hardware manufacturing.
